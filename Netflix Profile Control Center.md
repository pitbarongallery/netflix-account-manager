## 🎯 Overview

**Netflix Account Manager** is your complete guide to organizing and controlling every aspect of a Netflix subscription. Whether you're managing a family household, splitting costs among friends, or troubleshooting device limits, this guide walks through every option the platform offers.

Netflix has evolved from a simple streaming service into a sophisticated household management system with profiles, PINs, device limits, download restrictions, and household verification. This guide demystifies all of it.

## 🏠 Household Rules

> **📋 Netflix's Current Household Policy**
>
> Netflix defines a **"household"** as the people you live with. Devices outside your household may be required to verify periodically.
>
> **Key rules:**
> - 🏠 Household = devices on the same internet connection
> - 📍 Traveling? Use the "Watch temporarily" option
> - 🔐 Set a household via **Account → Household**
> - 📧 Verification email is sent when needed
> - 👨‍👩‍👧 Extra member slots are available on some plans

## 👥 Member Management

### 🪜 Adding an Extra Member

Available on Standard and Premium plans:

1. Go to **Account → Extra Members**
2. Click **Buy an extra member slot**
3. Enter email address
4. Member receives invite
5. They create their own login

## 🎭 Profile Management

### 🪜 Creating a Profile

1. **Account → Profiles → Add Profile**
2. Choose name and avatar
3. Toggle **Kids Profile** if desired
4. Set language and playback settings
5. Save

### 🎯 Profile Types

| Type | Features |
|------|----------|
| 👨 **Standard** | Full catalog |
| 👶 **Kids** | Curated, age-rated |
| 🎮 **Games** | Netflix games access |
| 🌍 **Regional** | Language-specific |

### 🔐 Profile PIN

Lock a profile so only you can use it:

1. **Account → Profiles → [Profile] → Profile Lock**
2. Enter Netflix account password
3. Set a 4-digit PIN
4. Require PIN for all content or mature content

### 🎨 Profile Customization

| Setting | Options |
|---------|---------|
| 🖼️ **Avatar** | 100+ icons |
| 🔤 **Language** | 30+ languages |
| ▶️ **Autoplay** | Next episode, previews |
| 🎬 **Playback** | Quality, subtitles |
| 📊 **Ratings** | Improve recommendations |

## 📱 Device Management

### 🪜 Viewing Signed-In Devices

**Account → Security → Manage Access & Devices**

Every device currently logged in appears here with:

- 📱 Device type
- 📅 Last activity
- 🌍 Approximate location
- 🗑️ Sign-out option

### 🔐 Remote Logout

Perfect for:

- 📱 Lost or stolen devices
- 🏠 Old roommates
- 🎓 College students moving out
- 🕵️ Suspicious activity

**Steps:**

1. Open **Manage Access & Devices**
2. Click **Sign Out** next to device
3. Confirm
4. Re-login required on that device

### 📊 Device Limits

| Plan | Simultaneous Streams |
|------|:--------------------:|
| 📺 Basic | 1 |
| 📺 Standard | 2 |
| 📺 Premium | 4 |
| 👑 Premium + Extra | 4 + members |

### ⚠️ Household Verification

If a device is outside your household, Netflix may request:

1. 📧 Email verification
2. 🌐 Same network connection
3. 📱 Code from account owner
4. ✅ Temporary access (14 days)

## 💬 FAQ

**How many people can share my account?**
Depends on plan — 2 to 4 simultaneous + extra members.

**Can I watch outside my household?**
Yes — for a limited time; then verification may be needed.

**How do I remove someone?**
Change password, then sign out all devices.

**Can I have multiple profiles?**
Yes — up to 5 per account.

**Does Netflix still offer free trials?**
Rarely — check current promotions.

**Can I download on multiple devices?**
Yes — up to your plan's limit.

**What's the cheapest plan?**
Basic with ads (where available).

**Can I split billing?**
No — one payment method per account.

**How do I cancel?**
Account → Membership → Cancel.

**Will I lose profiles on cancellation?**
They're restored if you reactivate within 10 months.
